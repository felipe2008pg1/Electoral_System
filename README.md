> **⚠️ NOTICE:** This repository and the **Electoral System V2** initiative have been officially cancelled, deprecated, and archived.

---

## 🛑 Why was this project discontinued?

To be brutally honest and straight to the point: **this repository became an unsustainable technical mess.** 

* **Legacy Code & Technical Debt:** The codebase grew excessively complex and difficult to maintain over time. Much of the older implementation lacked proper structure, modularity, and internal documentation, turning debugging or scaling into an absolute nightmare.
* **The Translation Trap:** The core logic was originally conceived and written entirely in Portuguese. When attempting to translate, refactor, and adapt everything to English midway through development, things became completely botched. The architecture ended up inconsistent, messy, and non-compliant with clean code standards.

Rather than wasting time trying to patch a sinking ship full of deep architectural flaws and broken translations, the most logical and professional path forward is to **scrap this repository completely and restart the system from scratch** under solid engineering principles.

---

## 🚀 What's Next & Future Outlook?

* **Clean Slate Architecture:** A future iteration of this system will be designed and built from the ground up, featuring proper architectural patterns, comprehensive documentation from day one, and a natively clean codebase written entirely in English from the very start.
* **Timeline:** There is **no fixed release date or timeline** for initiating or completing the new repository. It will be developed patiently when the right conditions and focus align.
* **Stay Updated:** Once there are concrete updates, architectural deep-dives, or the announcement of a brand new repository, I will share the updates on my official profile.
